# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. I will accept most things, as long as they do not
work against the goals of this project, its design principles, or the problems
it exists to solve.

All three are written down:

- [README.md](README.md) — what this is, and what it covers.
- [MOTIVATION.md](MOTIVATION.md) — the problems it exists to solve.
- [DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md](DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md) — the decisions everything else
  is built on.

The design principles are the binding ones. Each decision records what was
chosen, why, and the specific signal that would justify revisiting it, so read
them before changing anything structural. If you think a decision is wrong, say
so — open an issue naming the decision and what has changed — rather than
building against it.

This applies to anyone changing the project. It is not different for a person
and for an AI agent, and `CLAUDE.md` points here rather than restating it.
