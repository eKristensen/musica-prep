I have made a comprehensive search online to find every possible third party tool related to BluOS no matter what they promise to deliver, knowing well that many of them are not even close to fitting my requirements on their own. This appendix is a list of everything I have found that can in one way or another interact with BluOS players. I will not describe what they do here. Please visit the links of each project if you want to know more about any these projects.

(You might find even more projects here)[https://github.com/topics/bluos].
