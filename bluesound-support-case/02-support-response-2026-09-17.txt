Thank you for contacting The Bluesound Support Crew.


Regarding the issue, please try turning off mobile data/VPN/Cellular/mobile
data OFF. For an Android select Settings<Apps<BluOS to enable Location from
Apps and select BluOS.  You may choose to Allow only while using the app option
and let us know if there is an improvement in the players being discovered.

To help us better understand your issue, please provide the Diagnostic Log from
all of your Players.

To send the log:
- Using Desktop - In the BluOS Controller App, under the left Navigation
  Drawer, select Help and Send support request.
  https://support.bluos.net/hc/en-us/articles/360000205947
- Using a Mobile device -

  1. In the BluOS Controller app, tap the Home tab > Settings button.
  2. Under the Help & Support section, tap Send Support Request.
  3. Enter the required fields and tap Send Request. The diagnostic log of the
     player is generated and sent along with the support request.


Please include the "#560728" in the Subject field to help us identify your log,
and feel free to provide additional information in the Brief description of
this request.

The diagnostic log is included (automatically) with the request. Similarly,
repeat the steps mentioned above to send the diagnostic log of other players in
the BluOS Controller app.

Notes:
- Email address - Do NOT include spaces at the beginning or the end of your
  email address, as iOS devices place spaces automatically after a paste or
  autofill selection.
- Phone number - Do NOT include spaces in the phone number field. Type the
  number as "123456789" and not "123 456 789.

The logs provide us with detailed information to resolve the issue.
