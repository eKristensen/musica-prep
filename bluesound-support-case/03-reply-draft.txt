Hi Charmain,

Taking your suggestions in order:

Location: the BluOS app already had the location permission on both Android
phones, granted well before any of the testing I described, so that was already
in place when I wrote.

Mobile data: my two Android phones differ here — one has Wi-Fi only and no
cellular radio at all, the other has both. Both show the same behaviour, so
mobile data cannot be involved on the Wi-Fi-only phone. I have now tested it
explicitly on the phone that does have cellular: airplane mode on, Wi-Fi
switched back on, so Wi-Fi up with no mobile data. Four runs back to back, by
the same method as before, and the result was identical to my earlier numbers.

VPN: there is one on the phone with cellular, but it only carries traffic for
my local networks, not internet traffic. It was up during the tests I reported
earlier, so I have disabled it and run them again, including with airplane mode
on and Wi-Fi back on. Four runs, unchanged.

Diagnostic logs: you already have the log from the first player, and I am
sending the remaining three.

I would like to point out again, though, what the iPhone does on the same
network, through the same access point, to the same players. Discovery is the
part I have tested hardest there: cold start after cold start, all four players
appear at once and stay, every time, however often I restart the app. I have
not seen a single one of the discovery problems above on it.

I have also now tried grouping and ungrouping on the iPhone. That was a short
session rather than daily use, so I will not claim more than it showed, but I
could not provoke any of the grouping trouble I run into regularly on Android,
and it was noticeably smoother.

I left a few things out of my first message to keep it shorter. Here they are:

1) Occasionally the app opens with a message saying BluOS lost connection to my
player, and the normal interface is replaced by a full-screen discovery view. I
waited through it once: it found every player, slowly, one at a time, and then
the normal interface appeared, showing only the player I had selected before
closing the app. Everything the previous screen had just found was gone, and
had to be discovered again from scratch.

2) Sometimes the app comes back with a different player selected than the one I
was using when I closed it. Nothing I did changed the selection; it changes on
its own between one session and the next.

3) Ungrouping sometimes makes other players vanish from the list as well,
including players that were not part of the group I was changing.

4) A clarification on something I did mention. A player that is present and
playing sometimes vanishes from the list for a while and then returns, with no
action on my part. Even the currently selected player can disappear: while I am
still controlling it, with play, pause and volume all working, it is simply
absent from the list. So it is not a matter of the player being unreachable.

Finally, to restate the result I think is the most useful clue. Attaching a USB
Ethernet adapter to the phone, connected to the same layer 2 network as the
players, makes no difference on its own. Only when Wi-Fi is switched off as
well does the discovery time drop and the player list become reliable. A
virtualised Android instance, wired to that same network, is faster and
reliable in the same way — though still not in the same class as the iPhone
app, which is simply rock solid whatever I do to it.
