Hi Charmain,

Taking your suggestions in order:

Location: the BluOS app already had the location permission on both Android
phones, granted well before any of the testing I described, so that was already
in place when I wrote.

Mobile data: my two Android phones differ here — one has Wi-Fi only and no
cellular radio at all, the other has both. Both show the same behaviour, so
mobile data cannot be involved on the Wi-Fi-only phone. I have now tested it
explicitly on the phone that does have cellular: airplane mode on, Wi-Fi
switched back on, so Wi-Fi up with no mobile data. Four runs back to back, by
the same method as before, and the result was identical to my earlier numbers.

VPN: no VPN was active during any of the tests I have reported, before or now.

I have sent the diagnostic logs from the players. I would like to point out
again, though, that I also have an iPhone available, and on the same network
with the same players I see none of these problems there. I have now also tried
grouping and ungrouping on it briefly and could not provoke any of the grouping
trouble I run into regularly on Android; it was noticeably smoother. That was a
short test rather than long-term use, but the contrast was clear.

I left a few things out of my first message to keep it shorter. They are worth
including now, because none of them look like network problems:

1) Occasionally the app opens with a message saying BluOS lost connection to my
player, and the normal interface is replaced by a full-screen discovery view. I
waited through it once: it found every player, slowly, one at a time, and then
the normal interface appeared, showing only the player I had selected before
closing the app. Everything the previous screen had just found was gone, and
had to be discovered again from scratch.

2) Sometimes the app comes back with a different player selected than the one I
was using when I closed it. Nothing I did changed the selection; it changes on
its own between one session and the next.

3) Ungrouping sometimes makes other players vanish from the list as well, and
they need not have had anything to do with the group I was changing.

4) A clarification on something I did mention. A player that is present and
playing sometimes vanishes from the list for a while and then returns, with no
action on my part. Even the currently selected player can disappear: while I am
still controlling it, with play, pause and volume all working, it is simply
absent from the list. So it is not a matter of the player being unreachable.

Finally, to restate the result I think is the most useful clue, since it points
away from my network. Attaching a USB Ethernet adapter to the phone makes no
difference on its own. Only when Wi-Fi is switched off as well does the
discovery time drop and the player list become reliable. A virtualised Android
instance, wired to the same layer 2 network as the players, shows that same
fast and reliable result.
